## Shared role across papers

Each paper's accepted contribution must support the shared role. Members are
ordered by paper; the same paper_key identifies members from the same source
paper, including across the focus and candidates. A paper_key is not a paragraph
number, a matching signal, or an output handle.

Read each paper's accepted members together. Individual members may explain
different parts of one role, but every member must contribute directly. Do not
require each paragraph to answer the entire group question independently.
Every represented paper must support the role: do not assemble a supposed shared
answer using one part from one paper and an unrelated part from another.

For matching, the proposed addition must preserve this role across papers. If
the candidate's paper is already represented, consider its accepted members and
the proposed addition together, while requiring a direct contribution from the
candidate's own center. Do not count its unaccepted neighbors as members.
Read all source evidence even when the generated group question sounds suitable.
Never broaden a question to accommodate unrelated text.

Grouping by paper changes how accepted evidence is interpreted, not who can
join automatically. The caller determines membership.
